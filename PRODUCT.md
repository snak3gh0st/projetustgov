# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales executives (vendedores), coordinators, admins, CSM, and project specialists at PROJETUS — a Brazilian company that manages government contract leads (emendas parlamentares) and project execution via TransfereGov. Users work daily inside this dashboard: calling leads, updating statuses, tracking proposal pipelines, monitoring project execution deadlines, and managing commissions. Context is fast-paced, data-heavy; tasks require quick scanning across tables and cards.

## Product Purpose

Hub da PROJETUS is an internal CRM and operations platform for managing the full lifecycle of government contract instruments — from commercial lead acquisition through project execution and customer success. Success means users can find what they need, act on it fast, and trust the data they see.

## Brand Personality

Professional, decisive, high-trust. The product should feel like a well-engineered internal tool used by people who take government deadlines seriously — not a flashy startup product, not a generic enterprise SaaS. Precision without coldness.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream/purple/neon dashboards (HubSpot, Salesforce look-alike)
- Overly decorative admin panels with gradients on every surface
- Consumer-app soft pastels
- Heavy glassmorphism or translucent blur panels

## Design Principles

1. **Data first** — numbers and statuses are the content; chrome is the frame. Never let decoration compete with data.
2. **Instant orientation** — every screen tells you where you are and what to do next without reading. Hierarchy through spacing and weight, not labels.
3. **Trust through consistency** — same patterns, same colors, same behavior across all surfaces. Surprises cost trust.
4. **Economy of motion** — interactions are tools, not theater. Transitions confirm action, not entertain.
5. **Readable at a glance** — all text must be legible at the ambient contrast level for a busy open-plan office screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All text must pass 4.5:1 contrast in both light and dark modes. Focus rings visible. Touch targets minimum 44px. No color-only information encoding.
