# Roadmap: PROJETUS Transfer Gov Automation

## Overview

PROJETUS delivers 100% reliable automated extraction of Transfer Gov data through focused milestone delivery. Four milestones shipped: v1.0 (ETL pipeline), v2.0 (Streamlit dashboard, superseded), v3.0 (CRM de Vendas), v4.0 (Projetos em Execucao).
